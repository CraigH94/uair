Support

Questions, problems and ideas for Uair.

Get help

To report a problem, ask a question or suggest a feature, open an issue on GitHub. A free GitHub account is needed. Please include your macOS version, your Uair version (Uair → About Uair) and the steps that lead to the problem.

Open an issue on GitHub

Issues are public. Don't post personal information, and don't attach your data file or timesheets. If a problem depends on your data, say so and we'll find a way to reproduce it.

Your purchase

How does the free trial work?

Uair is free to download and to try for 14 days. Nothing is charged, then or later: when the trial ends, Uair stays read-only until you unlock it.

What does the unlock cost?

A one-time purchase of CHF 30, or the App Store's price in your country. There's no subscription, so there's nothing to cancel.

What happens if the trial ends without an unlock?

Nothing is deleted. You can still view and export all your recorded time, rename and file your timers, and shorten or delete sessions. Only recording new time needs the unlock.

How do I restore my purchase on another Mac?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple Account, then use Restore purchases in Uair's Settings → Purchase.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

Your data

Uair stores everything on your Mac, in ~/Library/Containers/io.github.craigh94.uair, and keeps daily backups there. To take your data elsewhere, export it as CSV or Excel with File → Export timesheet… (⌘E). See the Privacy Policy for details.
