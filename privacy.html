Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

At a glance

Uair collects no data. Everything you enter stays on your Mac, and nothing is sent to us.

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No network access in the App Store version
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple

Who we are

Uair is made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means the developer of Uair.

What Uair stores, and where

Your timers, projects, recorded time, rates and settings are saved on your Mac, inside Uair's own folder: ~/Library/Containers/io.github.craigh94.uair. Uair also keeps a few daily backups there, so you can recover from mistakes.

This data never leaves your Mac through Uair. The App Store version of Uair runs in the macOS App Sandbox without permission to use the network. When you export a timesheet, the file goes only where you choose to save it. If you back up your Mac with Time Machine, Uair's folder is included in that backup, under your control.

What Uair does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party code that collects data.
  • No cloud sync. We have no server and cannot see your data.

Purchases and payments

The trial and the unlock are sold and handled by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, e-mail address or payment details. To check whether your trial is running or Uair is unlocked, Uair asks Apple's StoreKit on your Mac and keeps the answer in its own folder. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy. Apple gives us only aggregated sales reports, which don't identify you.

Crash reports

If you have turned on Share with app developers in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and usage statistics for Uair with us. Apple provides these without your name or contact details, and you can turn this off at any time. Uair itself sends nothing.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, quit Uair, delete the app, and move the folder ~/Library/Containers/io.github.craigh94.uair to the Trash. There's no subscription to cancel.

Contacting us

Support happens through public issues on GitHub. Anything you post there is public and is handled by GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Please don't post personal information or your timesheet data.

This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, analytics or external fonts. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

Children

Uair collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. We will not start collecting data without saying so here first.
